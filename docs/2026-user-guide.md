## User Guide: how to calibrate Bartendro before serving your first robo-cocktail!

### BEFORE YOU BEGIN

* you have read the 2026-install-guide.md documentation
* you have a working Bartendro and have managed to connect to the Wi-Fi network it creates
* you have a tablet / phone / laptop that can be used to operate Bartendro
* you have bottles or jars of your favorite liquids (no carbonated drinks!)
* you have a bunch of thirsty friends and cups they can use
* you have read the rest of this documentation till the end


## CLEANING
* For sanitary reasons, prior to operating Bartendro you must clean the tubing with various rounds of soapy and clean water before loading the liquids you wish to serve as delicious robo-cocktails.
* Place Bartendro on a table/stable surface and fill one large jar/bottle with at least 1 litre of clean water
* Have a second large container or bucket located near the output of the dispensers to collect the water
* Connect Bartendro to power and turn the robot on, wait 2-3 minutes for the Pi to boot and network to appear
* Connect to the Wi-Fi network and navigate to `http://bartendro.local`
* Once the app is loaded, click/tap in the top-right hand corner (the bartendro logo)
* This will bring you to the admin login screen, input the username and password that you created during the installation or go to `http://bartendro.local/admin/lost-passwd` if you forgot it.
* Once you are in the admin backend, use the top navigation tabs select 'Dispensers'
* Within the Dispensers menu, scroll to the bottom to find the green 'clean' button.
* Ensure all the intake tubes of the dispensers are immersed into the water and that there is a container ready to receive the outflow - Bartendro will output a lot of volume in this process
* Click the button and wait for the clean cycle to finish - dump the water that passed through the machine, refill the container with clean water and a very small amount of dish-soap (emphasis on small)
* Run the clean cycle two more times with the lightly soapy water, then dump all the contents of the containers used and refill the intake one with clean water
* Run one last cycle with only clean water, to remove any soapy residue within the tubes.
* Empty the water containers and wipe the table/surface (it will get wet I promise you, have a towel at hand)

## SETUP 

* Place the bottles/jars with liquids where the clear tubing of each dispenser can reach the bottom of the bottle/jar
* Take a note of the maximum capacity of each container, the estimated current volume of liquids inside, and the pump number it is connected to
* Within the same Dispensers Menu you will need to select the liquid name (drop-down menu) and then add in the capacity and estimated current volume for each liquid you are serving.
* Once you go through all the dispenser pumps and have all the intake tubes within the bottles/jars you can now make a menu and do some testing
* To generate a new menu (which is what the humans will see on homepage of webapp), go to the 'Menu' tab within admin
* Select items from the extensive pre-installed selection of drinks or generate your own
* Be mindful that bartendro will only serve cocktails it has liquids for - if your dispensers have 'light rum' and the cocktail recipe requires 'dark rum' the robot will think it is out of rum.
