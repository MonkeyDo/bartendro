# Recommendations of ingredients for cocktails that work well with Bartendro

## Verbotten Flüssigkeit / DO NOT USE
- carbonated drinks such as soda water, tonic water, coca-cola, soft drinks - bubbles do not work well with the pumps and some of these liquids are corrosive to the delicate parts.
- Cream-based drinks such as  Kahula, Baileys, Coconut cream - they leaves sticky residue that rots the tubing
- Non-filtered aromatic water / infusions with sediments - particulates can get trapped in tubes or pumps
- Juices with pulp - whether hand-pressed or commercially bought - do strain out any pulp and fiber from juices

## Strong booze
- Whiskey
- Bourbon
- Light Rum
- Dark Rum
- Gin
- Vodka
- Tequila
- Mescal
- Triple Sec
- Absinthe


## other booze
- Campari
- Martini
- Amaretto
- Amaro
- Cognac
- Armaniac
- Drambuie
- Cointreu

## Mixers
- Grenadine
- Simple Syrup
- Cordial water / natural syrups
- Aperol
- Mint-infused water
- Kefir Lime infused water

## Juices 
- Orange
- Pineapple
- Cranberry
- Apple
- Lemon (hand-squeezed)
- Lime (hand-squeezed / caution: energy intensive)
